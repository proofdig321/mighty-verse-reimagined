# 03 — PRINCIPLES

The governing principles of Mighty Verse Reimagined. These constrain decisions but do not make them.

---

## Deliberate Tensions

The following are not problems to be solved. They are structural tensions that must be held
consciously and resolved only when the specific decision demands it.

`SOURCE` **Open vs Curated** — The degree to which the universe is open to external contribution
versus curated by Golden Shovel is unresolved.

`SOURCE` **Central Authority vs Decentralised Provenance** — Mighty Verse as contextual authority
coexists with the possibility of decentralised provenance. These are not mutually exclusive but
the boundary between them is unresolved.

`SOURCE` **Human Craft vs AI** — AI is an adaptation capability. The degree to which AI
participates in canonical production is unresolved.

`SOURCE` **Web2 vs Web3** — Both are valid delivery mechanisms. The balance, sequencing, and
dependency between them is unresolved.

`SOURCE` **Product vs Institution** — Whether Mighty Verse is primarily a product, a platform,
or a cultural institution is unresolved.

## Provenance and Rights

`SOURCE` The creative moment is canonical; interpretations may evolve.

`SOURCE` Mighty Verse remains the contextual authority over its universe.

`SOURCE` The Master is the canonical source. External platforms are projections.

`SOURCE` ISRC legitimacy — standard music industry rights identifiers are relevant to Mighty Verse, not only Web3 mechanisms.

`CANONICAL` **Authority** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Ultimate canonical authority is retained by Mighty Verse / Golden Shovel. It is non-transferable.

`CANONICAL` **Delegation** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Authority may be delegated over a Mural, a Song/World, a defined catalogue or body of work, a defined creative or contribution domain, or other explicitly bounded scopes. Delegation is scoped and revocable. Revocation does not erase history or invalidate work legitimately created while delegation was active. The authority status of affected works after revocation is preserved as historical lineage, not silently rewritten. Technical revocation mechanism is UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED.

`CANONICAL` **Attribution** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Meaningful creative contribution must remain attributable. Attribution covers all meaningful creative roles including: original artist/authorship, Director, collaborators, featured artists, Creative Moment creators, Interpretation creators, and other material contributors. Attribution is public by default for canonical creative relationships, subject to legitimate privacy/legal requirements. Attribution is correctable; corrections must preserve the historical fact that an earlier attribution existed. Silent overwriting of provenance history is not permitted. Attribution is not the same as ownership or canonical authority.

`CANONICAL` **Provenance** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Provenance applies to both the Master and projections. The Master requires provenance as the canonical source/state. Projections require provenance because the relationship between a projection and its canonical source must remain traceable. Provenance establishes lineage: canonical source → interpretation/derivative → projection/distribution. Provenance records must be publicly verifiable where the underlying information is legitimately public. Private/legal/identity details are not required to be public. Implementation mechanism (on-chain, off-chain, hybrid) is UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED.

`CANONICAL` **Rights** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Canonical status and rights ownership are separate concepts. Mighty Verse / Golden Shovel holds canonical authority over the universe, but rights in a work may involve artists, collaborators, featured artists, licensors, or other legitimate rights holders. An Interpretation creator may have rights in their Interpretation; the relationship between those rights and the underlying Creative Moment is UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED. A collector/owner of a projection does not automatically receive authorship, canonical authority, or underlying intellectual-property rights. Exact ownership, licensing, and royalty arrangements are UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED. Web2 vs Web3 rights mechanics are UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED.

`CANONICAL` **Integrity and Canonical Evolution** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
The canonical Master may be revised by the canonical authority when legitimately necessary. Revision must never destroy history. A revised Master creates a new canonical state in the lineage; the previous state remains historically attributable and traceable. Prior projections are not automatically erased by a subsequent Master revision; their relationship to the historical Master state is preserved. Whether a projection remains commercially or distributionally valid after a revision is a rights/business question and is UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED. Canonical evolution is possible; canonical history is not erased.

`CANONICAL` **Projection** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
A projection may change or become obsolete without rewriting the canonical history from which it originated.

`CANONICAL` **Collectibles are projections** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
The object of ownership is an authorised projection or representation of a canonical work — not the Master itself. A Card, Interpretation, Mural representation, edition, or other authorised manifestation may become a collectible projection. The Master / Creative Moment remains canonically controlled by Mighty Verse. A collector cannot modify the Master, claim authorship, or acquire canonical authority merely by owning a projection.

`CANONICAL` **Provenance link on collection** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
A collectible is cryptographically or economically identifiable as a projection of a particular canonical state. If the Master subsequently evolves, the collectible retains provenance to the canonical state from which it was issued. This connects Phase 3 canonical evolution directly into the ownership model: a projection's historical lineage is preserved regardless of subsequent Master revision.

`CANONICAL` **Ownership privileges** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Ownership of a collectible projection may confer additional access and recognition, including: higher-resolution or special projections; particular experiences; collector-only contextual material; participation in universe events; provenance and history views; early access to future projections; special interaction with a Creative Moment or Mural. Ownership does not confer authorship, canonical authority, or automatic intellectual-property rights in the underlying work.

`CANONICAL` **Collector status** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Ownership may confer universe-level recognition: a recognised collector identity, provenance-linked ownership history, collection displays, badges/status, participation history, and recognition associated with particular works or eras. Collector status is not social hierarchy over creators. Collector status ≠ creative authority.

`CANONICAL` **Free tier** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
There is a meaningful free layer. A person must be able to enter Mighty Verse, discover the universe, experience Creative Moments, explore provenance and context, view public projections, and participate in appropriate audience interactions without first purchasing anything or connecting a wallet. The participation model is: Discover → Experience → Participate → Collect → Deeper Access. Mighty Verse is not a paywall.

`CANONICAL` **Web3 is supplementary** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Web3 ownership is supplementary, not mandatory. A person may participate in Mighty Verse entirely through Web2 mechanisms. Web3 may provide: verifiable ownership; portable provenance; secondary transfer; programmable royalty and economic mechanisms; public verification; interoperability where appropriate. Wallet access must not become a prerequisite for experiencing Mighty Verse. Web2 and Web3 represent different ownership rails for the same underlying collectible model; the rights attached to each rail must be explicitly defined.

`CANONICAL` **Collectible object definition** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
A collectible is not the creative work. It is an authorised economic representation of a canonical state of the work. The following projection types are established as the initial collectible taxonomy:

- **Creative Moment projection** — a collectible representation of a canonical Creative Moment.
- **Card** — the primary/default collectible representation of a Creative Moment.
- **Interpretation** — a separately attributable creative interpretation of a Creative Moment; potentially collectible in its own right.
- **Mural representation** — a representation or access manifestation of a larger canonical experience.
- **Edition** — a defined issuance of a particular projection from a particular canonical state.
- **Other projections** — the taxonomy is open; future collectible forms must be possible without rewriting the constitution.

These types are not mutually exclusive. A single canonical Creative Moment may produce multiple collectible projections of different types, all pointing back to the same canonical lineage without becoming the same object.

`CANONICAL` **Separation of economic concepts** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
The following concepts are distinct and must not be collapsed into a single token, contract, or data model:

- **Canonical authority** — held by Mighty Verse / Golden Shovel; non-transferable.
- **Creative authorship** — attributable to creators and contributors; separate from ownership.
- **Provenance** — lineage of canonical state and projections; must remain traceable.
- **Ownership** — holder of a particular collectible projection.
- **Economic entitlement** — revenue or royalty rights legitimately carried by a collectible or participant.
- **Access** — what the holder can experience.
- **Web3 representation** — optional technical rail for representing or verifying some of the above.

This separation must be preserved through all subsequent economic, technical, and product decisions.

`CANONICAL` **Economic provenance chain** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Every economic event must be capable of referencing: collectible → projection → canonical state → provenance lineage → economic event. The economic layer must not become a disconnected marketplace database. Economic events are anchored to canonical provenance, not merely to token or asset identifiers.

`CANONICAL` **Collectible designation** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Mighty Verse explicitly designates which projections are collectible. A projection is not collectible merely because it exists or is distributed. Mighty Verse deliberately authorises a projection as collectible, attaching that designation to a specific canonical state and projection. Experiential, distributional, archival, and collectible projections may therefore coexist without every projection becoming an owned asset.

`CANONICAL` **Multiple collectibles per Creative Moment** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
One canonical Creative Moment may produce multiple distinct collectible objects and experiences. The same canonical lineage may support a Card, limited Edition, authorised Interpretation, Mural representation, or future projection types. These are distinct owned objects with their own identities, provenance, and entitlement bundles, while all remaining linked to the same canonical lineage.

`CANONICAL` **Interpretation economic identity** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
An Interpretation is both a projection/derivation of the underlying Creative Moment and potentially a work with its own economic identity, depending on how it was created and authorised. Where an Interpretation contains meaningful independent creative contribution and is appropriately authorised, the Interpretation creator may have separable economic rights. Creation of an Interpretation does not transfer canonical authority over, or ownership of, the underlying Creative Moment. An Interpretation may have its own attribution, provenance, and economic waterfall while remaining subordinate to the canonical lineage from which it derives.

`CANONICAL` **Collectible entitlement bundle** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Ownership means ownership of the designated collectible projection together with the specific entitlements attached to that collectible. The entitlement bundle may include: collectible identity; projection type; canonical-state reference; provenance reference; edition/issuance information; defined access privileges; collector recognition/status; defined transfer rights; explicitly defined economic entitlements. Ownership does not automatically include: authorship; canonical authority; ownership of the Master; underlying IP rights; unrestricted reproduction rights; unrestricted commercial exploitation rights. Different collectible types may have different entitlement bundles.

`CANONICAL` **Collectible issuance principle** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Mighty Verse does not sell or transfer the underlying canonical work merely by issuing a collectible. It issues defined economic representations of canonical creative states, with explicitly bounded ownership and entitlement.

`CANONICAL` **Platform economic model** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Mighty Verse is a media and cultural platform first. The collectible economy is an additional economic layer, not the definition of the platform. Users may discover, consume, follow, participate, and engage without ever becoming collectors. The platform operates multiple legitimate economic channels simultaneously:

1. **Consumption / advertising economics** — free access to core content; a native Mighty Verse advertising interface as part of the free/consumption experience; sponsored content or experiences where appropriate; revenue generated through audience consumption.
2. **Collectible economics** — authorised Cards, Editions, Interpretations, Mural representations, and future collectible types; primary issuance; secondary transfer where permitted; entitlements attached to each collectible.
3. **Creator economics** — revenue participation arising from consumption; revenue participation arising from collectible issuance; appropriate collaborator and featured-artist participation; Interpretation creator economics where applicable.
4. **Mighty Verse platform economics** — advertising; platform and distribution services; collectible issuance; other legitimate platform revenue.
5. **Web3 economics** — optional ownership, provenance, and transfer infrastructure; supplementary to the broader Web2 media and platform experience; wallet ownership must not be required for ordinary Mighty Verse consumption.

No single channel is assumed to be primary. The revenue waterfall per channel and the participation of each economic actor within each channel are UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED.

`CANONICAL` **Consumption / advertising economics** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Where consumption can be attributed to a specific Creative Moment, Song, World, Interpretation, or other canonical/authorised work, the relevant creator and legitimate contributors may participate in the economics generated by that consumption. Participants may include: canonical creator; collaborators/contributors; featured artists; Interpretation creator where the consumed work is an Interpretation; Mighty Verse as platform. Not every advertising impression requires attribution to an individual work. Mighty Verse may maintain platform-level advertising revenue for activity that cannot reasonably be attributed to a specific work. The model supports both work-attributed consumption economics and platform-level advertising economics. There is no requirement that every advertising event be artificially mapped to a Creative Moment.

`CANONICAL` **Primary collectible revenue** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Primary collectible revenue participates according to the provenance, authorship, and economic identity of the collectible being issued. Participants may include: canonical creator/legitimate rights participants; collaborators; featured artists; Interpretation creator where applicable; Mighty Verse; other legitimately established participants. The waterfall may vary by collectible type and by the economic identity of what is being issued. Primary economics must remain traceable to: collectible → projection → canonical state → provenance/attribution → economic waterfall.

`CANONICAL` **Secondary transfer economics** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
An authorised secondary transfer may create an economic event beyond the seller. Where a collectible has secondary economic participation attached to it, the transfer may generate defined participation for: creator/rights participants; collaborators/featured artists where applicable; Interpretation creator where applicable; Mighty Verse; potentially other participants defined by the collectible's entitlement bundle. Secondary participation is not automatically identical for every collectible. It is determined by the authorised economic terms attached to the collectible/projection and may be: inherent to a designated collectible class; absent from another class; or specifically defined in an individual collectible's entitlement bundle. The existence of a secondary economic entitlement must be clear at issuance.

`CANONICAL` **Interpretation creator economics** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
An independently authorised Interpretation may generate independent economics for its creator while retaining economic linkage to the underlying Creative Moment. Where the Interpretation has independent creative/economic identity: the Interpretation creator receives the economic entitlement established for that Interpretation; the underlying Creative Moment/canonical creator may retain economic participation where the authorisation/economic structure provides for it; Mighty Verse may participate in the Interpretation economic layer as platform; an Interpretation may be independently issued as a collectible with its own primary and secondary economic structure. The Interpretation does not become economically detached from its provenance merely because it has independent economic identity. Its economics remain traceable to: underlying Creative Moment → Interpretation → collectible/economic event.

`CANONICAL` **Variable waterfalls by collectible type** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Different collectible types may have different revenue waterfalls. There is no constitutional requirement that Card, Edition, Interpretation, and Mural representation are economically identical. Each collectible class may have its own entitlement and waterfall provided that: the terms are defined; the relevant participants are attributable; the provenance is preserved; the collector's entitlement is clear before acquisition; no collectible silently transfers canonical authority or underlying IP ownership.

`CANONICAL` **Collector economic participation** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Collector economic participation is an optional entitlement, not an automatic consequence of ownership. A collector does not automatically receive downstream revenue merely by purchasing a collectible. However, Mighty Verse may deliberately issue collectibles whose entitlement bundle includes defined economic participation — potentially including secondary transfer participation, defined downstream consumption participation, or other explicitly authorised economic participation. Where such participation exists, it must be an explicit property of the collectible or collectible class. Ownership ≠ automatic economic participation. Ownership may include economic participation where explicitly issued as part of the entitlement bundle.

`CANONICAL` **Mighty Verse platform share** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Mighty Verse participates economically in the channels where it provides the platform, infrastructure, distribution, marketplace, issuance, or other enabling service, subject to the economics defined for that channel. Its platform participation may exist across: consumption/advertising; primary collectible issuance; secondary transfer; Interpretation economics; other authorised platform economic activity. Mighty Verse is not required to take an identical share from every channel. Mighty Verse may participate economically as the platform without becoming the owner or author of the underlying creative work. This preserves the distinction between platform economics, creative rights, canonical authority, and ownership.

`CANONICAL` **Attribution-aware economic model** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
The Mighty Verse economic model is attribution-aware but not artificially attribution-bound. Where economic activity can meaningfully be connected to a particular work, projection, Interpretation, or collectible, the relevant provenance and attribution can determine participation. Where economic activity is genuinely platform-level, Mighty Verse may operate a platform-level economic pool. No percentage, royalty rate, payment schedule, blockchain mechanism, or token standard is established by these decisions.

`CANONICAL` **Economic calculation model** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Mighty Verse maintains reusable economic rules operating at multiple levels: platform/channel level; collectible class level; individual object level where specifically required. The default is configurable per collectible class, while individual collectibles may carry specific economic terms at issuance. Consumption/advertising economics operate under a separate calculation model from collectible economics. For work-attributed consumption, participation is calculated from the economic value attributable to the relevant work. For genuinely unattributed platform advertising revenue, the default is Mighty Verse platform revenue unless a separate allocation rule has been explicitly established. Unattributed advertising revenue is not automatically redistributed to creators.

`CANONICAL` **Participant entitlement resolution** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Economic entitlement is governed by the economic terms applicable to the specific work or collectible at the time the economic event occurs, subject to any terms permanently attached at issuance. For collectibles, issuance-attached economic terms are authoritative for that collectible. For consumption/advertising activity, the applicable economic rule for that channel and period governs. Where multiple participant types exist on the same work, entitlements are calculated independently from the applicable gross/net economic basis according to each participant's defined entitlement rule, unless the attached economic rule explicitly defines a sequential waterfall. The economic model supports both independent entitlement calculations and explicitly defined sequential waterfalls.

`CANONICAL` **Waterfall configuration** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Mighty Verse maintains reusable, named waterfall definitions as economic records/configurations rather than embedding economic rules in application behaviour. A waterfall definition may specify: participating roles; entitlement basis; calculation method; applicable economic channel; applicable collectible class; ordering where a sequential waterfall is required; effective date; version; and other conditions required to calculate the entitlement. Waterfalls are attachable to a work, projection, collectible class, or individual collectible. The platform resolves an economic event by reading the applicable economic rule. Economic rules are data-driven and versioned; the application provides the resolution and enforcement mechanism.

`CANONICAL` **Primary vs secondary waterfall** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Primary issuance and secondary transfer may use separate waterfall definitions because they represent different economic events. A collectible may carry a primary issuance waterfall, a secondary transfer waterfall, and additional economic rules where legitimately required. The economic obligations attached to a collectible at issuance remain authoritative for that collectible unless the collectible's own terms explicitly permit a later change. A later change to Mighty Verse's default waterfall must not silently rewrite the economics of previously issued collectibles. A collectible permanently carries or references its historical economic terms in the same way its provenance remains historically traceable.

`CANONICAL` **Consumption / advertising settlement** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Where revenue can be meaningfully attributed to a specific work through consumption activity, creator/participant economics are calculated from the revenue attributable to that work. Direct attribution is used where available; advertising events are not artificially converted into a broader weighted creator pool when direct attribution exists. For the platform-level pool of genuinely unattributed advertising revenue, Mighty Verse retains that revenue as platform revenue unless a separate allocation rule has been explicitly established. A separate allocation mechanism may be established in the future but must be an explicit economic rule, not an automatic assumption.

`CANONICAL` **Settlement lifecycle** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
The economic model supports the following settlement states: Calculated — entitlement determined, not yet payable; Accrued — accumulating toward a defined settlement condition; Payable — threshold or settlement period met; Settled — payment successfully completed; Held — settlement temporarily prevented by validation, dispute, compliance, provenance, attribution, or other legitimate condition; Reversed — original economic position cancelled or corrected through a subsequent economic event. A minimum settlement threshold and/or settlement period may be configured. An entitlement that has not yet reached its settlement condition remains historically recorded and is not erased.

`CANONICAL` **Economic history** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Every calculated economic entitlement must be historically reproducible from the inputs that produced it. The historical economic record must preserve or permanently reference: the economic event; the relevant source; provenance applicable at the time; attribution applicable at the time; participant identities/roles; the waterfall or economic rule used; the version/effective state of that rule; the calculation basis; the resulting entitlement; subsequent settlement events. If an economic rule changes, the change applies to future economic events unless an existing entitlement explicitly permits otherwise. Historical economic events remain governed by the rule that applied to them. A new rule creates a new economic state/version rather than silently rewriting previous calculations.

`CANONICAL` **Economic corrections and reversals** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
Corrections are new economic events, not silent amendments to historical economic records. The original economic event and its original calculation remain preserved. A correction, dispute resolution, reversal, refund, attribution correction, or other adjustment creates a subsequent event that changes the resulting economic position. The model supports the trace: original economic event → original entitlement → correction/reversal event → adjusted entitlement → settlement state. Where attribution or provenance is later corrected, the correction may affect future economic calculations and may create compensating adjustments for affected historical events, but the original historical calculation is never silently erased. Economic history may be corrected, but it must not be silently rewritten.

`CANONICAL` **Economic engine principles** (2026-08-17, founder-established)
The Mighty Verse economic engine is: configurable; versioned; provenance-aware; attribution-aware; historically reproducible; correction-preserving. Economic rules are records/configurations rather than hidden hard-coded business logic. Collectibles retain their issuance-attached economic terms. Consumption/advertising uses its own attribution-aware model. Primary and secondary economics may use different waterfalls. Settlement has explicit lifecycle states. Corrections create new economic events rather than destroying the historical record. No blockchain, chain, token standard, smart-contract architecture, wallet model, payment provider, database schema, or API design is established by Phase 4C.
