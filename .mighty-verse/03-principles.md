# 03 — PRINCIPLES

The governing principles of Mighty Verse Reimagined. These constrain decisions but do not make them.

---

## Deliberate Tensions

The following are not problems to be solved. They are structural tensions that must be held
consciously and resolved only when the specific decision demands it.

`SOURCE` **Open vs Curated** — The degree to which the universe is open to external contribution
versus curated by Golden Shovel is unresolved.

`SOURCE` **Central Authority vs Decentralised Provenance** — Mighty Verse as contextual authority
coexists with the possibility of decentralised provenance. These are not mutually exclusive but
the boundary between them is unresolved.

`SOURCE` **Human Craft vs AI** — AI is an adaptation capability. The degree to which AI
participates in canonical production is unresolved.

`SOURCE` **Web2 vs Web3** — Both are valid delivery mechanisms. The balance, sequencing, and
dependency between them is unresolved.

`SOURCE` **Product vs Institution** — Whether Mighty Verse is primarily a product, a platform,
or a cultural institution is unresolved.

## Agent and AI Principles

`CANONICAL` Do not invent missing product, domain, technical, economic, or cultural decisions.

`CANONICAL` Do not begin implementation merely because context exists.

`CANONICAL` If information is not established by source material, mark it UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED.

`CANONICAL` The old Mighty Verse implementation is historical evidence, not binding architecture.

`CANONICAL` Platform Core and its agents are external infrastructure. They must not be automatically inherited into Mighty Verse decisions.

`CANONICAL` Agents propose. Humans approve. Agents cannot override the constitution.

`CANONICAL` No agent should automatically deploy to production without human sign-off.

`CANONICAL` The Master MCP exists to protect Mighty Verse's meaning from implementation drift.

`CANONICAL` Context loss is architecture debt, not merely documentation debt.
