# 01 — SOUL

The irreducible identity of Mighty Verse. What it is before it is anything else.

---

## What Mighty Verse Is

`SOURCE` Mighty Verse is a canonical cultural universe.

`SOURCE` Mighty Verse gives African creative culture a canonical home.

`SOURCE` Golden Shovel is the originating artist and owner of that universe.

`SOURCE` Mighty Verse is not fundamentally an NFT platform, hologram platform, streaming platform,
animation platform, or music platform. These are mediums and mechanisms through which the deeper
model can be expressed.

`SOURCE` The deeper model is a structured, navigable, attributable cultural universe.

---

## The Master / Source Relationship

`SOURCE` The Master is the canonical source.

`SOURCE` External distribution platforms are projections of the source, not the source itself.

`SOURCE` Mighty Verse remains the contextual authority over its universe.

---

## Core Creative Units

`SOURCE` A song is a world.

`SOURCE` A Mural is the complete expression of that world.

`SOURCE` A Card is a meaningful creative moment within that world.

`SOURCE` The creative moment is canonical; interpretations may evolve.

---

## What Mighty Verse Is Not

`SOURCE` Mighty Verse is not defined by any single delivery mechanism.

`SOURCE` The old Mighty Verse implementation is historical evidence, not automatically the
architecture of Mighty Verse Reimagined.
