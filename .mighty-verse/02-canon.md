# 02 — CANON

The canonical concepts of Mighty Verse and their authoritative definitions.

---

## Canonical Entities

`CANONICAL` **Golden Shovel** — The founding artist and platform owner of the Mighty Verse universe.

`CANONICAL` **Mighty Verse** — The canonical cultural universe owned and defined by Golden Shovel. The canonical source, not a streaming service.

`CANONICAL` **Song / World** — A Song is a World. The Song itself is the World within Mighty Verse — the experiential and canonical universe embodied by that Song. These are not two separate entities. Do not create a separate World entity unless a later founder decision explicitly requires one.

`CANONICAL` **Mural** — The complete visual/spatial expression of a Song/World. Has a Director.

`CANONICAL` **Director** — The creative authority over a Mural.

`CANONICAL` **Creative Moment** — A meaningful unit within a World. May be represented as a Card. Its canonical identity belongs to the World, not to any particular Mural. Distinguish between: (1) the canonical Creative Moment, (2) its appearance/expression within a Mural, (3) its possible Interpretations, (4) projections of those canonical objects to external experiences.

`CANONICAL` **Card** — A representation of a Creative Moment.

`CANONICAL` **Interpretation** — A Creative Moment may have multiple Interpretations. Different interpretation styles represent legitimate cultural perspectives. Three interpretation styles are confirmed to exist; their exact names and definitions are UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED.

`CANONICAL` **Master** — The canonical source and state of the creative work, including its identity, meaning, and authoritative relationships. Not reducible to a media file or storage asset. The original Master remains at Mighty Verse; external platforms receive projections.

---

## Core Ontology Principle

`CANONICAL` (2026-08-17, founder-established)

> Mighty Verse owns the canonical meaning and source relationship of the creative universe. Experiences, interpretations, media representations, distribution channels and ownership mechanisms may project from that canonical source without becoming the source itself.

---

## Canonical Hierarchy

`CANONICAL` (2026-08-17, founder-established)

```
Song / World
  └── Mural  (complete visual/spatial expression of the World)
        └── Creative Moment / Card  (meaningful unit; canonical identity belongs to the World)
              └── Interpretation  (legitimate cultural perspective on a Creative Moment)
```

A Creative Moment's canonical identity belongs to the World, not to a Mural.
A Mural is one expression of the World; it does not own the Creative Moments that appear within it.

---

## Projection Boundary

`CANONICAL` (2026-08-17, founder-established)

A projection is an experience, representation, distribution, or derivative expression of the Master.

A projection may vary in: format, resolution, presentation, device, interface, delivery mechanism, experiential context, and commercial/ownership mechanism where legally and constitutionally permitted.

Those variations do not automatically alter the canonical Master.

Rights, attribution, provenance and ownership remain separate domain questions — not assumed to vary freely.

## Participant Model

`CANONICAL` (2026-08-17, founder-established)

The following are established conceptual participant categories. Exact role taxonomy, eligibility mechanics, and access rules remain open unless stated below.

**Golden Shovel** — Originating artist, platform owner, and ultimate canonical authority. The universe is expandable beyond Golden Shovel, but Golden Shovel's founding authority is not transferable.

**Artist / Creator** — Mighty Verse is architecturally expandable beyond a single artist. Golden Shovel remains the originating authority. Other creators may participate within the universe. The exact model for how other artists create within the universe is UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED.

**Director** — Holds scoped creative authority over a specific Mural. The Director role is not restricted to Golden Shovel; it can be held by another person. A Director's authority is scoped to their Mural and does not transfer ultimate canonical authority.

**Audience** — Participants who experience the universe. Audience participation does not automatically imply canonical authority. The universe must not become fundamentally paywalled; access should not differ fundamentally based solely on whether someone is a viewer, collector, or contributor. Different capabilities may exist across roles, but the universe itself remains accessible.

**Interpretation creator** — A conceptual category for participants who create Interpretations of Creative Moments. Eligibility is restricted to designated/eligible participants, not automatically open to everyone. Exact eligibility mechanism is UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED. An Interpretation does not need to become canonical to be legitimate — legitimacy and canonicity are distinct.

**Contributor** — A conceptual category for participants who contribute material. Public contribution is treated as contribution or interpretation rather than automatic creation of canonical Creative Moments. Canonical authority over new Creative Moments remains controlled.

**Collector / Owner** — A participant who holds an economic or legal interest in a collectible projection (a Card, Creative Moment representation, edition, or other authorised manifestation). Ownership attaches to the projection, not the Master. Ownership does not confer authorship or canonical authority. Ownership may confer additional access, recognition, and privileges as defined by the collectible's terms. Web2 and Web3 ownership rails are both valid; exact mechanics per rail are UNKNOWN / TO BE ESTABLISHED.

## Canonical Authority Model

`CANONICAL` (2026-08-17, founder-established)

- Ultimate canonical authority resides with Mighty Verse / Golden Shovel and is not transferable.
- Canonical authority can be delegated in defined circumstances. Delegation is scoped — it does not transfer ultimate authority.
- A Director's creative authority over a Mural is an existing example of scoped delegation.
- Audience participation, ownership, and collection do not automatically confer canonical authority.
- Interpretations are legitimate cultural perspectives; legitimacy does not require canonicity.



`SOURCE` 2.5D is not merely a visual gimmick. It is the idea of creative moments becoming spatial objects.

`SOURCE` Two legitimate media creation paths exist:

**Path 1 — Native Mighty Verse production**
The animator supplies layers directly: background, midground, foreground, depth.

**Path 2 — Legacy / external media adaptation**
The creator supplies existing video, image, artwork, or archive material.
Mighty Verse may progressively derive depth, segmentation, spatial layers, objects, and anchors.
AI extraction is an adaptation capability for this path.

`SOURCE` AI layer extraction is an adaptation capability for legacy media, not a mandatory production assumption.
